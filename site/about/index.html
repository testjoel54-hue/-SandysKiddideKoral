Hello, Meno!

A familiar name with a little sparkle.

Sandy's Kiddie Korral is a center-based childcare provider in Meno, Oklahoma. The center's public childcare record lists care for infants, toddlers, preschoolers and school-age children.

On our logo, you'll find the words Childcare • Preschool • Transportation - the three topics that bring many families to us. For specific services, routes or openings, we recommend calling the center.
