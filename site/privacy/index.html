Your information matters.

Here's how the contact inquiry form on this website is designed to handle the details you share.

This privacy notice covers the contact form and information on this website. It is written for the website build and should be reviewed by the childcare center before publishing.

Information you choose to send

The contact form asks for your name, email, optional phone number, optional child age group, topic and message. Please do not submit sensitive medical, payment or identification information in this form.

How messages are delivered

When configured by the site owner, submissions are passed through a Vercel serverless endpoint to Web3Forms so the center can receive your request by email. The site owner must configure the recipient email and verify that its privacy policy and retention practices suit their needs.

Why contact information is used

The information you submit is intended to help the childcare center respond to your inquiry. Submission does not guarantee childcare placement, a tour or enrollment.

Website browsing

This build does not include advertising pixels or a dedicated analytics script. The site hosting platform may process technical access data to deliver the website and protect against abuse.

Contact and corrections

For questions about the information you supplied or a request to correct it, contact the center at (580) 776-2221 or at the contact details published on its Oklahoma state listing.

Before launch, the owner should review this notice, confirm who receives enquiries, and add any additional legally required policies based on actual practices.
