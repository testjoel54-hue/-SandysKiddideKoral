Sandy's Kiddie Korral · Meno, Oklahoma

Little questions. Helpful answers.

Here are the public facts about our center, plus the topics worth discussing directly before you decide on care.

For parents & guardians

All your questions belong here.

We want families to have the facts they need. When a detail cannot be verified online, we'll encourage you to check with the center.

What are the childcare center's hours?

The Oklahoma Child Care Locator lists Monday through Friday from 5:30 AM to 6:00 PM. Saturday and Sunday are closed. Please call to confirm holiday schedules.

What ages does Sandy's Kiddie Korral accept?

The state listing includes infants (0-11 months), toddlers (12-23 months), preschool-aged children (2-4 years), and school-age children (5 years and older).

Does the center accept childcare subsidy?

Yes. The Oklahoma state listing indicates that the center accepts subsidy. Ask the center directly about your eligibility and how enrollment works.

Is transportation offered?

The Sandy's Kiddie Korral logo mentions transportation. Contact the center for confirmation of current routes, eligibility, hours, and availability.

Can I schedule a visit?

Call (580) 776-2221 or submit the inquiry form to ask whether a visit can be arranged. A request does not automatically confirm a tour or place.

How much does childcare cost?

Tuition and fees were not supplied or verified for this website. Please contact the center for current pricing and any applicable subsidy information.

Who should I contact for more information?

The Oklahoma listing identifies Sandra Weathers as director and lists (580) 776-2221 and weathers79@gmail.com as contact details.
